What We Do

Technical Services & Solutions

From formulation development to on-site assistance, we provide technical services designed to support your production process and finishing requirements.

01

Custom Formulation Development

Developing and adjusting color and topcoat formulations to match customer specifications, leather characteristics and requested samples.

02

Technical Tannery Visits

Our technical specialists can visit your tannery in Robbiki or other locations across Egypt to assist with on-site applications and production challenges.

03

Testing & Performance Evaluation

Evaluating leather performance through abrasion, flexibility and water-resistance testing to support product quality and application requirements.

04

Color Matching

Supporting the development and adjustment of leather colors to achieve the desired shade, appearance and finishing effect.

05

Production Process Assistance

Helping customers review application procedures, identify technical issues and improve consistency throughout leather finishing operations.

06

Research & Development

Exploring and improving formulations and application solutions to respond to changing customer requirements and industry needs.

Laboratory & Quality Evaluation

Performance Matters. Every Detail Counts.

Our technical evaluation approach focuses on understanding how leather performs under different application and usage requirements.

Abrasion Resistance

Evaluating surface durability and resistance to rubbing and wear.

Flexibility Testing

Assessing finish flexibility and its ability to withstand repeated movement.

Water Resistance

Evaluating leather finish performance against water exposure.

Color Evaluation

Reviewing color appearance and matching requirements against requested samples.

Surface Finish Evaluation

Assessing surface appearance and finishing characteristics for different applications.

Quality Assessment

Reviewing technical requirements and supporting consistent product evaluation.
