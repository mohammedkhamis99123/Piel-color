WHO WE ARE

A Rich Heritage in Leather Chemicals

PIEL COLOR is one of the established companies in the development and manufacture of integrated chemical solutions for leather tanning, processing, and finishing. Founded in Spain, the company has expanded its technical services across Europe, Africa, and the Middle East.

Through our factory and headquarters in Quesna, Menoufia, Egypt, we provide technical support to local and regional tanneries, preparing and formulating chemicals to meet the needs of the footwear, handbag, furniture, and automotive upholstery markets.

Advanced Laboratories

Color matching, formulation, and sample testing at our Quesna facility.

Reliable Delivery

Comprehensive service for tanneries in Robbiki and across Egypt.
